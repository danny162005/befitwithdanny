About befitwithdanny

Hey, I'm Dnyanesh Kolekar!

Welcome to my fitness community. My own journey in the gym started five years ago. While I hold a B.Com degree, my true passion has always been health, lifting, and pushing past physical limits. Three years ago, I realized that my biggest motivation wasn't just my own progress, but helping others achieve theirs. That's when I officially transitioned into coaching.

I believe in real results backed by real science. To make sure I am providing my clients with the best possible guidance, I earned three distinct certifications from MIFI:

  • 🏆 Certified Fitness Trainer
  • 🥗 Certified Nutrition Specialist
  • 💊 Certified Supplement Specialist

Today, I'm incredibly grateful to share my workouts, nutrition tips, and daily life with a family of over 46,000 of you on Instagram. Whether you want to build muscle, drop body fat, or just feel healthier and more confident, I'm here to give you the blueprint.

Let's build your best self, together!
